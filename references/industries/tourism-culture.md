# Industry Pack — Tourism & Culture

**Slug:** `tourism-culture`
**Use for:** Travel agencies, museums, cultural centers, tour operators, destinations

## Visual codes

- Destination photography full-bleed, coordinate/location strings
- Itinerary numbering, map motifs, local pattern traditions
- Bilingual headlines when market requires (Arabic + English for Gulf)

## Palette tendencies

- Earth tones from destination: sand `#E8DCC8`, ocean `#1A535C`, sunset accent
- Photography-driven — UI recedes, imagery leads
- Local craft patterns as subtle texture overlay

## Avoid

- Generic globe + airplane clip art
- Stock "tourist with suitcase" photos
- Same layout as hospitality restaurant sites

## Typography (vetted pairings)

1. Fraunces + Work Sans — editorial travel
2. Playfair Display + Source Sans 3 — luxury destination
3. Cormorant Garamond + Lato — cultural heritage

## Signature scene ideas

- Horizontal scroll destination gallery
- Scrollytelling: day-by-day itinerary
- Pinned crossfade: destination highlights

## Imagery keywords

`Oman landscape`, `desert travel`, `cultural heritage`, `coastal destination`
Picsum IDs: 1015, 1018, 1025

## Copy voice

Evocative, inviting, specific to place. Name real locations, seasons, experiences.
"Plan your trip" / "View packages" / "Contact guide".

## Concept examples

- **Destination atlas** — full-bleed chapters per location
- **Journey manifest** — itinerary as numbered scroll narrative
- **Heritage trail** — cultural sites with coordinate flourish

## One action default

Plan trip / Book tour
