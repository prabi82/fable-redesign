# Industry Pack — Education

**Slug:** `education`
**Use for:** Schools, universities, training centers, tutoring, ed-tech, courses

## Visual codes

- Campus photography, library textures, learning pathway numbering
- Achievement stats (students, years, programs) with tabular numerals
- Calendar/schedule panels for enrollment periods

## Palette tendencies

- Academic navy `#1B3A5C`, warm gold accent (school colors from brand), cream `#FAF8F5`
- Youth-oriented: brighter accent from existing brand, white ground
- Avoid corporate cold gray unless university institutional

## Avoid

- Childish clip art; cartoon mascots unless brand has one
- SaaS purple gradients for traditional schools
- Dense text blocks without hierarchy

## Typography (vetted pairings)

1. Libre Baskerville + Karla — traditional academic
2. Fraunces + Work Sans — modern learning
3. Outfit + Source Serif 4 — contemporary institution

## Signature scene ideas

- Timeline: institution history
- Horizontal gallery: campus life, facilities
- Data-panel hero: enrollment dates, program count

## Imagery keywords

`university campus`, `classroom learning`, `library students`, `graduation`
Picsum IDs: 305, 307, 399

## Copy voice

Inspiring, clear, inclusive. Programs and outcomes in plain language.
"Apply now" / "Download prospectus" / "Contact admissions".

## Concept examples

- **Academic registry** — indexed programs, proof-first credentials
- **Campus chronicle** — editorial chapters per faculty/department
- **Learning pathway** — scroll-drawn progress through curriculum stages

## One action default

Apply / Contact admissions
