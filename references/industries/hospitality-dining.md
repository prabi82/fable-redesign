# Industry Pack — Hospitality & Dining

**Slug:** `hospitality-dining`
**Use for:** Restaurants, cafes, catering, banquet halls, heritage dining, food brands

## Visual codes

- Architectural arch frames, ornamental rules, warm food texture
- Chapter-book editorial structure, heritage dates ("Est. 1985")
- Local script calligraphic accents (Arabic thuluth hints, not clipart)
- Table settings, flame, spice, marble, brass — sparingly as accents

## Palette tendencies

- Warm creams `#FDF6EE`, deep espresso `#2C1810`, brass/gold accent ONLY if existing brand uses gold
- Heritage luxury: dark moody + serif justified when brand is already dark/gold
- Modern casual: warm white ground + one rich accent (terracotta, sage, burgundy)

## Avoid

- Generic dark+gold when client brand is light
- Stock food that's clearly not their cuisine
- Emoji food icons; carousel sliders for menu items

## Typography (vetted pairings)

1. Cormorant Garamond + Lato — heritage editorial
2. Playfair Display + Source Sans 3 — classic fine dining
3. Fraunces + Work Sans — contemporary bistro

## Signature scene ideas

- Pinned crossfade: menu chapters (Appetizers → Mains → Desserts)
- Scrollytelling: dish stories with full-bleed food photography
- Scroll-drawn: ornamental arch path connecting sections

## Imagery keywords

`heritage restaurant interior`, `Omani cuisine`, `banquet hall`, `chef plating`, `warm dining room`
Picsum IDs: 292, 312, 326, 431, 488

## Copy voice

Warm, confident, sensory. Short lines. "Reserve your table" not "Book now". Heritage claims as facts, not fluff.

## Concept examples

- **Editorial chapter book** — oversized serif, arch-framed images, numbered sections
- **Kitchen chronicle** — open-kitchen energy, bold grotesk, high-contrast food photography
- **Palace dining** — only when brand warrants: ornament, bilingual headlines, ceremonial pacing

## One action default

Book a table / Reserve a hall / Call to inquire
