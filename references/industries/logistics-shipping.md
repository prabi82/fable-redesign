# Industry Pack — Logistics & Shipping

**Slug:** `logistics-shipping`
**Use for:** Freight forwarders, shipping agencies, port services, customs, supply chain

## Visual codes

- Voyage manifests, port codes, container grids, route lines on scroll
- Monospace coordinates, vessel tickers, bill-of-lading typography
- Nautical charts, crane silhouettes, container color blocks (not clipart)

## Palette tendencies

- Institutional navy `#003466`, signal orange or teal accent, light ice-blue grounds
- Corporate light themes preferred when existing site is light
- Dark nautical ONLY when brand already dark maritime

## Avoid

- Gold/luxury serif (that's hospitality, not shipping)
- Purple tech gradients
- Handshake stock photos
- Generic "global logistics" globe icons

## Typography (vetted pairings)

1. Syne + IBM Plex Sans — industrial modern
2. Space Grotesk + Source Sans 3 — manifest/data feel
3. Archivo + Bitter — corporate precise

## Signature scene ideas

- Scroll-drawn route line between ports
- Data-panel hero: live departures, rates, transit times
- Horizontal gallery: fleet, ports served, container types

## Imagery keywords

`container ship`, `port crane`, `cargo terminal`, `freight logistics`, `Gulf port`
Picsum IDs: 1050, 1051, 1059

## Copy voice

Precise, confident, operational. Numbers and timelines upfront. "Request a quote" / "Track shipment".

## Concept examples

- **Gulf Port Manifest** — monospace port codes, route ticker, light navy corporate
- **Container registry** — indexed service list, bento grid of capabilities
- **Transit ledger** — timeline of shipment stages with scroll-drawn connector

## One action default

Request a quote / Contact operations
