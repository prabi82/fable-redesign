# Industry Pack — Personal Services

**Slug:** `personal-services`
**Use for:** Salons, spas, gyms, fitness studios, beauty, personal training, coaching

## Visual codes

- Before/after or transformation narrative (tasteful, not gimmicky)
- Service menu as editorial list, not price grid
- Booking panel, hours, practitioner credentials

## Palette tendencies

- Soft neutrals + one signature accent from brand (rose, sage, charcoal)
- Wellness: calm teals and warm whites
- Fitness: high contrast, bold accent, energetic photography

## Avoid

- Medical clinic aesthetics for beauty brands (unless medical spa)
- Aggressive discount banners
- Stock gym bro photography

## Typography (vetted pairings)

1. Cormorant Garamond + Lato — spa/luxury beauty
2. Outfit + Source Sans 3 — modern fitness
3. Fraunces + Work Sans — wellness coaching

## Signature scene ideas

- Data-panel hero: book appointment, today's hours
- Horizontal gallery: services, facility, team
- Scrollytelling: service experience journey

## Imagery keywords

`spa wellness`, `fitness studio`, `salon interior`, `personal training`
Picsum IDs: 837, 849, 978

## Copy voice

Personal, aspirational, specific. Name services and outcomes.
"Book your session" / "View services" / "Call salon".

## Concept examples

- **Ritual menu** — services as editorial chapters, not card grid
- **Studio chronicle** — trainer/therapist profiles with sticky imagery
- **Wellness sanctuary** — calm textures, soft motion, appointment CTA persistent

## One action default

Book session / Call to book
