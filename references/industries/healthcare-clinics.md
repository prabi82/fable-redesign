# Industry Pack — Healthcare & Clinics

**Slug:** `healthcare-clinics`
**Use for:** Clinics, hospitals, dental, wellness centers, medical practices

## Visual codes

- Clean clinical whitespace, soft rounded corners (not pill-everything)
- Trust signals: credentials, specialties, appointment availability
- Calm photography — never graphic medical imagery on marketing pages

## Palette tendencies

- Soft teal `#2A9D8F`, calm blue `#457B9D`, warm white `#FAFCFB`
- One reassuring accent — avoid alarming red except emergency CTAs
- Light themes strongly preferred

## Avoid

- Dark luxury aesthetics
- Stock photos of surgery or blood
- Aggressive sales language
- Dense information walls without hierarchy

## Typography (vetted pairings)

1. Outfit + Source Sans 3 — clean clinical
2. DM Sans + DM Serif Display — trustworthy modern
3. Work Sans + Fraunces — warm approachable

## Signature scene ideas

- Data-panel hero: hours, specialties, book appointment
- Scrollytelling: patient journey steps
- Proof-first: credentials and specialties band

## Imagery keywords

`medical clinic interior`, `doctor consultation`, `wellness calm`, `healthcare professional`
Picsum IDs: 846, 849, 865

## Copy voice

Reassuring, clear, empathetic. Plain language. "Book an appointment" / "Call clinic".

## Concept examples

- **Care pathway** — numbered steps, calm photography, appointment CTA persistent
- **Specialty index** — clean list with credentials, not three equal cards
- **Wellness sanctuary** — soft textures, spa-adjacent for wellness brands only

## One action default

Book appointment / Call clinic
