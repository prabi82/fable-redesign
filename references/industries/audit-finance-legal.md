# Industry Pack — Audit, Finance & Legal

**Slug:** `audit-finance-legal`
**Use for:** Auditors, accountants, law firms, financial advisors, compliance, tax

## Visual codes

- Ledgers, hairline rules, tabular numerals, accreditation seals
- Institutional blue scales, stamp/watermark motifs, archive filing metaphors
- Credentials strip: CMA, IAPA, ISO badges as proof-first content

## Palette tendencies

- Deep navy `#001878`, mid blue `#003466`, ice blue `#E8F0FA` grounds
- Light institutional themes — existing audit firms are almost always light
- Accent: one saturated blue or restrained teal — never gold luxury

## Avoid

- Dark moody + gold serif (instant wrong industry read)
- Celebration/handshake stock imagery
- Playful rounded UI; crypto/Web3 aesthetics
- Three equal feature cards with circle icons

## Typography (vetted pairings)

1. Libre Baskerville + Karla — institutional gravitas
2. DM Serif Display + DM Sans — modern professional
3. Instrument Serif + Instrument Sans — contemporary audit

## Signature scene ideas

- Proof-first credentials band immediately after hero
- Scrollytelling: services with sticky document imagery
- Timeline: firm history since est. date

## Imagery keywords

`modern corporate office`, `city skyline professional`, `documents abstract`, `glass building`
Picsum IDs: 145, 148, 149

## Copy voice

Authoritative, clear, trustworthy. No hype. Credentials and years of experience as facts.
"Schedule a consultation" / "Speak with an advisor".

## Concept examples

- **Audit ledger** — tabular data, proof-first rhythm, clean sans body
- **Compliance archive** — hairline grid, numbered service index
- **Advisory chamber** — restrained serif headlines, credentials as hero proof

## One action default

Schedule consultation / Contact office
