# Industry Pack — Manufacturing & Industrial

**Slug:** `manufacturing-industrial`
**Use for:** Factories, industrial suppliers, OEM, fabrication, heavy industry

## Visual codes

- Machinery photography, process flow diagrams, capacity metrics
- Monochrome + one safety/industrial accent (orange, yellow sparingly)
- ISO/certification badges as proof content

## Palette tendencies

- Steel gray `#4A5568`, industrial blue `#1E3A5F`, off-white `#F0F0EE`
- Dark factory floor photography with light text overlays for hero
- Accent: brand color or safety orange — one only

## Avoid

- Luxury hospitality gold/serif
- Soft wellness pastels
- Cartoon factory icons

## Typography (vetted pairings)

1. Archivo + Bitter — industrial editorial
2. Space Grotesk + IBM Plex Sans — technical modern
3. Syne + Source Sans 3 — bold manufacturing

## Signature scene ideas

- Scroll-drawn process line (raw → finished)
- Horizontal gallery: facilities, machinery, products
- Proof-first: certifications and capacity stats

## Imagery keywords

`manufacturing floor`, `CNC machinery`, `industrial warehouse`, `steel production`
Picsum IDs: 1050, 1059, 1060

## Copy voice

Capable, precise, scale-focused. Tonnage, capacity, certifications as facts.
"Request a quote" / "Contact sales".

## Concept examples

- **Production line** — numbered process stages with scroll connector
- **Capacity registry** — indexed capabilities, bento by sector
- **Factory floor** — full-bleed industrial photography, minimal overlay type

## One action default

Request quote / Contact sales
