# Industry Pack — Retail & E-commerce

**Slug:** `retail-ecommerce`
**Use for:** Shops, boutiques, product brands, online stores, showrooms

## Visual codes

- Product photography hero, lifestyle context shots, category navigation
- Price/offer typography with tabular numerals
- Seasonal campaign bands, collection labels

## Palette tendencies

- Brand colors from existing identity — often bold primary + neutral ground
- Minimal white product pages OR warm lifestyle tones
- One accent for CTAs — "Add to cart" / "Shop collection"

## Avoid

- Generic Shopify template look (equal product grid filling page)
- Fake sale countdown timers unless real
- Purple AI ecommerce clichés

## Typography (vetted pairings)

1. Outfit + Source Sans 3 — clean retail
2. Syne + IBM Plex Sans — bold contemporary
3. DM Sans + DM Serif Display — lifestyle brand

## Signature scene ideas

- Horizontal scroll product gallery
- Off-grid collage hero with featured products
- Sticky scrollytelling: collection story

## Imagery keywords

`product flat lay`, `boutique interior`, `lifestyle shopping`, `brand packaging`
Picsum IDs: 225, 267, 369

## Copy voice

Direct, benefit-led, sensory for lifestyle brands. "Shop the collection" / "Visit store".

## Concept examples

- **Lookbook editorial** — full-bleed lifestyle, minimal UI chrome
- **Collection index** — asymmetric bento by category importance
- **Showroom visit** — location-first for brick-and-mortar retail

## One action default

Shop now / Visit store
