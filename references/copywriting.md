# Copywriting Guide

Rewrite flabby corporate prose into short, confident editorial lines. Real content only.

## General rules

1. **Lead with outcome**, not company history (unless heritage IS the story)
2. **One idea per sentence.** Max 2 lines per paragraph on marketing pages
3. **Active voice.** "We audit" not "Auditing services are provided"
4. **Specific beats vague.** "Est. 1990 in Ruwi" not "Decades of experience"
5. **No filler:** leverage, synergy, world-class, cutting-edge, passionate about

## Before → after patterns

| Before (corporate slop) | After (editorial) |
|-------------------------|-------------------|
| We are a leading provider of comprehensive logistics solutions | Gulf freight forwarding from Muscat since 1987 |
| Our team of dedicated professionals is committed to excellence | 40 auditors. CMA registered. Ruwi, Muscat. |
| Welcome to our website | {Delete — start with value proposition} |
| Click here to learn more | View services |
| Submit | Request a quote |

## Microcopy by function

| Element | Pattern | Examples by industry |
|---------|---------|-------------------|
| Primary CTA | Verb + object | Request quote / Book table / Schedule consultation |
| Secondary CTA | Explore pattern | View services / Our halls / See projects |
| Form success | Confirm + next step | Received. We'll respond within one business day. |
| Form error | Specific fix | Enter a valid phone number including country code |
| Nav labels | Nouns, ≤2 words | Services, About, Contact — not "Our Services Page" |
| Section kickers | Uppercase, tracked | EST. 1990 · MUSCAT · CMA REGISTERED |

## Industry voice quick reference

- **Hospitality:** sensory, warm — "Slow-roasted over charcoal"
- **Logistics:** operational, precise — "Port Salalah to Jebel Ali, 36 hours"
- **Audit/legal:** authoritative, calm — "Statutory audit for Omani LLCs"
- **Healthcare:** reassuring, plain — "Same-week appointments available"
- **Construction:** capable, factual — "12 commercial towers delivered since 2015"
- **Tech/SaaS:** outcome-led — "Ship features weekly, not monthly"

## Bilingual / RTL (Gulf clients)

- Design headlines for both scripts from the start
- Arabic often needs 20–30% larger line-height than Latin
- Mirror layout under RTL; don't just flip text
- Bilingual kickers: `EN · AR` or side-by-side with equal visual weight
- CTA in primary market language; secondary language as subtitle option

## Headline formulas

1. **Heritage + place:** "Muscat's table since 1985"
2. **Capability + proof:** "Auditors to Oman's leading groups"
3. **Action + benefit:** "Move cargo across the Gulf — one contact"
4. **Question (sparingly):** "Planning an audit this quarter?"

## What to extract from existing site

Copy the facts, rewrite the prose:
- Service names (keep exact legal names)
- Credentials, memberships, registrations
- Addresses, phones, emails, hours
- Heritage dates, parent company relationships
- Client industries served (if listed)

Never invent services, awards, or statistics not found in research.
